## **Can International Students Work in AI Jobs in the USA While Studying?**

Yes. International students in F-1 status may be able to gain practical experience in Artificial Intelligence (AI), software development, machine learning, data science, and related fields through authorized employment opportunities.

However, F-1 students cannot simply accept any internship or job because it is related to technology. They must understand the employment authorization rules that apply to their situation.

Two important types of practical training are:

- **Curricular Practical Training (CPT):** Allows eligible students to participate in qualifying practical training that is an integral part of their academic curriculum, before completing their program.

- **Optional Practical Training (OPT):** Allows eligible F-1 students to gain practical experience directly related to their major, either before or after completing their studies.

Students who graduate with qualifying STEM degrees may also be eligible for an additional 24-month STEM OPT extension.

For someone pursuing an AI career in the USA, understanding these options is important before accepting an internship, starting a project with a company, or applying for a full-time position.

This guide explains CPT and OPT, the differences between them, how they apply to AI jobs, what employers should know, and how international students can prepare for employment while maintaining F-1 status.

## **1. What Are CPT and OPT?**

CPT and OPT are practical-training options available to eligible F-1 international students. Both allow students to gain experience related to their academic field, but they serve different purposes.

### **What is Curricular Practical Training (CPT)?**

Curricular Practical Training is a form of practical training that takes place before a student\'s program end date.

It may involve an internship, cooperative education opportunity, or another qualifying practical experience offered through the student\'s academic program.

CPT must:

- Be an integral part of an established curriculum.

- Be directly related to the student\'s major area of study.

- Meet applicable federal requirements and the university\'s academic requirements.

- Be authorized by the student\'s DSO in the Student and Exchange Visitor Information System (SEVIS).

- Be approved for the specific employer and period of employment.

A student must receive CPT authorization before beginning work. The authorization is recorded on the student\'s Form I-20.

### **What is Optional Practical Training (OPT)?**

Optional Practical Training allows eligible F-1 students to gain practical experience directly related to their major field of study.

Unlike CPT, OPT does not have to be an integral part of the established curriculum.

OPT may be available:

- Before completing the academic program (pre-completion OPT).

- After completing the academic program (post-completion OPT).

The DSO recommends OPT in SEVIS, but U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) makes the employment authorization decision. Students generally need an approved Employment Authorization Document (EAD) before starting OPT employment.

### **CPT vs. OPT: Key Differences**

  ------------------------------------------ --------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------------------------------

The biggest distinction is that CPT is part of qualifying academic practical training, while OPT provides a separate practical-training opportunity that does not have to be built into the curriculum.

## **2. Can AI Students Get Internships Through CPT?**

Yes, if the internship satisfies CPT requirements and the student\'s DSO authorizes it.

For example, a student pursuing a qualifying Computer Science or Artificial Intelligence degree may receive an internship opportunity involving:

- Machine learning model development.

- Generative AI application development.

- Data engineering.

- Software engineering.

- Natural language processing.

- Computer vision.

- AI research and experimentation.

But the fact that an internship is related to AI does not automatically make it eligible for CPT.

The academic structure of the internship matters.

### **Important 2026 CPT clarification**

On August 24, 2026, the Student and Exchange Visitor Program (SEVP) issued additional guidance concerning CPT authorization.

The clarification emphasizes that CPT must be an integral and required part of an established curriculum. It is not intended to be used simply to provide an optional employment or internship opportunity.

For example, an elective course that a student chooses solely to obtain an internship opportunity may not satisfy the applicable requirements. University guidance following the clarification emphasizes that the practical-training requirement must apply to students participating in the same program, regardless of citizenship.

Students should therefore avoid assuming that a university\'s internship option, elective course, or employment arrangement automatically qualifies for CPT.

**What this means for AI students:** Before accepting an internship, ask the university\'s international student office whether the internship is genuinely part of the established curriculum and whether the school can authorize it under current CPT rules.

### **Example: AI engineering internship**

Imagine an international student pursuing a master\'s degree in Computer Science.

The student receives an internship offer from a company developing AI-powered software. The role involves:

- Building Python applications.

- Integrating large language models.

- Developing RAG pipelines.

- Evaluating AI application performance.

- Working with cloud-based AI services.

The role may be academically relevant. However, the student must also satisfy the CPT curriculum requirements and obtain authorization from the DSO.

The student cannot begin work simply because the company has issued an offer letter.

## **3. What Are the Eligibility Requirements for CPT?**

CPT eligibility depends on the student\'s academic circumstances, the proposed training, and the institution\'s requirements.

Generally, an eligible student must:

1.  Be enrolled at an SEVP-certified college or university.

2.  Have completed at least one full academic year of lawful full-time study, unless an applicable exception applies.

3.  Be enrolled in a full course of study.

4.  Have secured a qualifying practical-training opportunity.

5.  Participate in practical training that is integral to the established curriculum.

6.  Ensure the training is directly related to the student\'s major.

7.  Receive authorization from the DSO before beginning employment.

### **Is there an exception to the one-academic-year requirement?**

Yes. Certain graduate programs that require immediate participation in practical training may qualify for an exception to the normal one-full-academic-year requirement.

This is not a general exception for every master\'s student. The program must meet the applicable conditions, and the DSO must determine eligibility.

### **Can CPT be part-time or full-time?**

Yes. CPT can be authorized on a part-time or full-time basis, depending on the academic program and the authorization.

  --------------- -----------------------------
   **CPT type**        **General meaning**

   Part-time CPT    20 hours per week or less

   Full-time CPT   More than 20 hours per week
  --------------- -----------------------------

The specific authorization will identify the employer and the permitted dates.

### **Does CPT require an EAD?**

No. CPT authorization is recorded on the student\'s Form I-20 by the DSO. Students do not normally need to file Form I-765 with USCIS for CPT.

However, this does not mean students can begin working immediately after receiving an offer. They must wait until the CPT authorization is issued and the authorized start date has arrived.

## **4. What Is Day 1 CPT, and Why Should Students Be Careful?**

Day 1 CPT is a term commonly used to describe academic programs that allow eligible graduate students to participate in CPT from the beginning of their studies.

Some graduate programs may qualify for an exception to the one-academic-year requirement when immediate practical training is required by the curriculum.

However, the exception is not a general permission for universities to offer immediate employment authorization to every new international student.

Under the 2026 SEVP clarification, students should be especially careful about programs that appear to use CPT primarily as a way to provide employment opportunities.

Before enrolling in a program marketed as Day 1 CPT, investigate:

- Whether the practical training is genuinely required by the curriculum.

- Whether the requirement applies to all students in the program, regardless of citizenship.

- Whether the university can explain its academic basis for the training.

- Whether the program satisfies applicable SEVP requirements.

- Whether the university\'s international student office has clearly explained the authorization process.

- Whether the course schedule and practical training requirements are academically credible.

A university\'s advertising claim that it offers Day 1 CPT does not guarantee that an individual student\'s proposed employment will qualify.

Students should obtain independent, qualified advice if they have concerns about a program\'s compliance or the impact on their future immigration options.

## **5. Does Using CPT Affect Your Future OPT Eligibility?**

It can.

One of the most important rules for international students is the effect of full-time CPT on post-completion OPT.

An F-1 student who accumulates **12 months or more of full-time CPT at the same educational level becomes ineligible for post-completion OPT at that level**.

Part-time CPT does not trigger this restriction. Less than 12 months of full-time CPT also does not trigger the one-year bar.

### **Example 1: Part-time CPT**

A student completes 10 months of part-time CPT during a master\'s program.

The student does not lose post-completion OPT eligibility merely because of this part-time CPT participation. Other OPT requirements still apply.

### **Example 2: Less than 12 months of full-time CPT**

A student completes eight months of full-time CPT.

The student does not trigger the one-year full-time CPT bar. The student may still be eligible for post-completion OPT, subject to all other requirements.

### **Example 3: 12 months or more of full-time CPT**

A student completes 12 months of full-time CPT at the master\'s degree level.

The student becomes ineligible for post-completion OPT at that same educational level under the applicable rule.

  -------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------------------------------------------
          **CPT participation**                                 **Effect on post-completion OPT**
              Part-time CPT                                     Does not trigger the one-year bar
   Less than 12 months of full-time CPT                         Does not trigger the one-year bar
    12 months or more of full-time CPT    Makes the student ineligible for post-completion OPT at that educational level
  -------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This is an important consideration for students who plan to use CPT during their AI master\'s program and then seek full-time AI employment through OPT.

Do not choose a CPT arrangement without understanding how the accumulated full-time authorization may affect your future options.

## **6. What Is OPT, and How Can AI Graduates Use It?**

Optional Practical Training is particularly important for international students who want to transition from academic study into full-time employment.

After completing an eligible academic program, students may apply for post-completion OPT to work in positions directly related to their major.

For example, graduates may pursue roles such as:

- AI Engineer.

- Machine Learning Engineer.

- Generative AI Engineer.

- Data Scientist.

- Data Engineer.

- Software Engineer.

- Computer Vision Engineer.

- AI Research Engineer.

The actual job duties must relate directly to the student\'s major field of study.

### **How long does OPT last?**

Eligible students may generally receive up to 12 months of OPT at each qualifying higher educational level.

For example:

  --------------------- -----------------------------
   **Education level**   **General OPT opportunity**

   Bachelor\'s degree          Up to 12 months

    Master\'s degree           Up to 12 months

     Doctoral degree           Up to 12 months
  --------------------- -----------------------------

Eligibility and previous OPT participation can affect the available period.

Students who have completed a qualifying STEM degree may be eligible for an additional 24-month STEM OPT extension, subject to the relevant requirements.

### **Does OPT require a job offer before applying?**

For the initial post-completion OPT period, students generally do not need to secure a job offer before the DSO can recommend OPT.

However, students must obtain the required USCIS employment authorization and EAD before starting work.

Once authorized, the employment must be directly related to the student\'s major, and students must comply with the applicable unemployment limits and reporting requirements.

## **7. How Can AI Students Apply for OPT?**

The process involves the university\'s international student office and USCIS.

A general sequence is:

**Step 1: Contact your DSO**

Discuss your program completion date and the appropriate OPT application timeline.

**Step 2: Request an OPT recommendation**

The DSO reviews your eligibility and enters the OPT recommendation in SEVIS.

**Step 3: Receive an updated Form I-20**

Your university provides an updated I-20 reflecting the OPT recommendation.

**Step 4: File Form I-765**

Submit Form I-765, Application for Employment Authorization, to USCIS with the required documentation and applicable fee.

**Step 5: Wait for USCIS approval**

Do not start OPT employment merely because you have submitted the application.

**Step 6: Start work only when authorized**

You may begin working only after the required employment authorization is in effect and the authorized start date has arrived.

### **When should students apply for post-completion OPT?**

Under the general application rules, students may apply up to 90 days before their program completion date and no later than 60 days after completing the program.

The application must also be filed within 30 days of the DSO entering the OPT recommendation in SEVIS.

These deadlines are important. Students should coordinate the process with their university rather than waiting until after graduation.

## **8. Can AI Graduates Get STEM OPT After Regular OPT?**

Yes, if they meet the additional STEM OPT requirements.

Students with qualifying STEM degrees may apply for a 24-month extension of post-completion OPT.

This can provide up to 36 months of post-completion practical training in total, combining the initial 12-month OPT period and the additional 24-month extension.

### **Main STEM OPT requirements**

A student generally needs to:

- Have a qualifying STEM degree included on the DHS STEM Designated Degree Program List.

- Be in a valid period of post-completion OPT.

- Have employment directly related to the qualifying STEM degree.

- Work for an employer enrolled in E-Verify.

- Complete the required Form I-983 training plan with the employer.

- Meet the applicable employment, reporting, and application requirements.

- Receive the necessary DSO recommendation and USCIS authorization.

### **What is Form I-983?**

Form I-983 is the Training Plan for STEM OPT Students.

It documents the relationship between the student\'s academic background and the practical training provided by the employer.

It includes information about:

- The student\'s learning objectives.

- The practical skills the student is expected to develop.

- The employer\'s training and supervision arrangements.

- How the employer will evaluate the student\'s progress.

- How the work relates to the student\'s STEM degree.

The student and employer must work together to complete the required plan.

## **11. What Should Employers Know About Hiring F-1 Students?**

Companies hiring international students should understand the difference between CPT, OPT, and STEM OPT.

The requirements are not interchangeable.

  ------------------------ ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   **Employment pathway**                                       **Employer considerations**
            CPT                         Student must have school-authorized CPT for the specific employer and dates
        Initial OPT                    Student must have the appropriate USCIS employment authorization and valid EAD
          STEM OPT          Employer must meet additional requirements, including E-Verify enrollment and Form I-983 obligations
  ------------------------ ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

For STEM OPT, employers must also provide appropriate supervision and training and comply with the relevant reporting and evaluation requirements.

Companies should not assume that a student\'s F-1 status alone permits employment.

### **Questions employers can ask candidates**

- Do you currently have CPT or OPT authorization?

- What are the authorized employment dates?

- Does your current authorization cover this specific employer and role?

- If you are on STEM OPT, is the employer eligible to participate?

- Are there any training-plan or reporting requirements the employer must support?

Employers should use their legal and HR teams to verify documentation and compliance rather than relying solely on informal statements from candidates.

## **15. Frequently Asked Questions About CPT and OPT for AI Jobs**

### **1. Can F-1 students work in AI internships in the USA?**

Yes, if they have the appropriate employment authorization. A qualifying internship may be authorized through CPT or another applicable pathway, depending on the student\'s circumstances and academic program.

### **2. Can I get CPT for a Generative AI internship?**

Potentially. The internship must satisfy the CPT curriculum requirements and be directly related to your major. Your DSO must authorize it before you begin working.

### **3. Can I work full-time on CPT?**

Yes, full-time CPT may be possible when authorized by the DSO and permitted by the academic program. However, 12 months or more of full-time CPT at the same educational level makes a student ineligible for post-completion OPT at that level.

### **4. Does part-time CPT affect OPT eligibility?**

Part-time CPT does not trigger the one-year full-time CPT bar. Other OPT eligibility requirements still apply.

### **5. Can I start working after receiving a CPT offer letter?**

No. You must receive the CPT-authorized Form I-20 and wait until the authorized employment start date.

### **6. Do I need an EAD for CPT?**

No. CPT authorization is recorded on the student\'s Form I-20 by the DSO. CPT does not normally require a separate EAD from USCIS.

### **7. Can I apply for OPT without a job offer?**

For initial post-completion OPT, students generally do not need a job offer before the DSO can recommend OPT. However, they must obtain USCIS employment authorization before beginning work.

### **8. Can I work as an AI Engineer on OPT?**

Yes, if the position is directly related to your major field of study and you meet all applicable OPT requirements.

### **9. Can I work for multiple employers on OPT?**

Generally, yes, provided the employment meets the applicable OPT requirements and remains directly related to your major. Students must maintain appropriate records and comply with reporting requirements.

### **10. Does every AI master\'s degree qualify for STEM OPT?**

No. The specific degree\'s CIP code must meet the DHS STEM designation requirements. The degree title alone is not enough.

### **11. Can I get 36 months of work authorization through OPT and STEM OPT?**

Eligible students may generally receive up to 12 months of post-completion OPT followed by an additional 24-month STEM OPT extension. The extension is conditional and must be separately applied for and authorized.

### **12. Does OPT guarantee an H-1B visa?**

No. OPT and STEM OPT provide temporary practical-training authorization under F-1 rules. H-1B is a separate employment-based nonimmigrant classification with its own requirements and selection process.

### **13. Can I use CPT during my first semester?**

Only if the student and program qualify for an applicable exception to the normal one-academic-year requirement. The exception is not automatic, and the DSO must determine whether the proposed training qualifies.

### **14. Should I choose a university based on CPT availability?**

Do not make the decision based only on CPT availability. Evaluate the academic curriculum, degree classification, accreditation, faculty, projects, career support, tuition, and the university\'s compliance practices.
