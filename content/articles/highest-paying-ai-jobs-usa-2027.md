# **Highest-Paying AI Jobs in the USA in 2027: Salaries, Skills and Career Opportunities**

A career in artificial intelligence can pay well. But the biggest salaries are not necessarily attached to the job titles that receive the most attention on social media.

An experienced machine learning engineer building systems used by millions of customers may earn more than someone with a fashionable AI title and limited production experience. A research scientist working on foundation models may command a very different compensation package from an AI product manager at a smaller company.

There is another detail worth understanding: **salary figures depend heavily on how they are measured.**

A government wage statistic, a recruiter\'s starting-salary estimate and a software engineer\'s reported total compensation are three different things. Comparing them without context can give job seekers a misleading picture of the market.

This article looks at some of the highest-paying AI-related careers in the United States, using available salary evidence and explaining what the numbers actually represent.

## **How much do AI professionals earn in the USA?**

There is no single official salary database that ranks every AI job title. The US Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) tracks established occupations, but newer titles such as Generative AI Engineer, Agentic AI Engineer and Forward Deployed Engineer do not always have their own occupational categories.

For a more useful comparison, this article draws on three sources:

- **BLS:** Official national median wages for established occupations, based on May 2025 data.

- **Robert Half:** 2026 starting-salary ranges for technology roles, based on its salary guide.

- **Levels.fyi:** Employee-reported compensation data, including base salary, annualized stock and bonuses.

These sources answer different questions. BLS figures represent national occupational wage medians. Robert Half provides starting-salary estimates. Levels.fyi reports total compensation from submitted salary data, which can be particularly relevant when comparing technology-company offers.

## **1. AI / Machine Learning Engineer**

\<badge color=\"success\"\>Strong salary benchmark\</badge\>

AI and Machine Learning Engineers develop software systems that use machine learning to make predictions, identify patterns, automate decisions and solve complex problems.

They might build fraud detection systems for banks, recommendation engines for online retailers or computer vision applications for manufacturing companies.

At senior levels, the work often extends beyond model development. Engineers may be responsible for architecture, infrastructure, performance, deployment and the reliability of AI systems used by customers.

**US salary data**

Robert Half\'s 2026 Salary Guide gives AI/ML Engineers the following national starting-salary estimates:

  ------------------ ----------------------------
   **Salary level**   **Annual starting salary**

         Low                  \$134,000

         Mid                  \$170,750

         High                 \$193,250
  ------------------ ----------------------------

These are Robert Half estimates for starting salaries, not BLS national median wages or guaranteed offers.

Levels.fyi reported a median total compensation of \$156,000 for AI Engineers in the United States as of November 1, 2026. Its reported total compensation includes base salary, annualized stock and bonus.

**Skills that influence compensation**

- Python, C++ or other relevant programming languages

- Machine learning and deep learning

- PyTorch and TensorFlow

- Model evaluation and optimization

- Data engineering fundamentals

- Cloud infrastructure

- Production deployment and monitoring

**Who should consider this role?**

Software engineers, computer science graduates and professionals with strong programming and mathematical foundations who want to work on production AI systems.

## **2. AI Research Scientist**

AI Research Scientists work on advancing the technology itself. Their responsibilities may include developing new model architectures, improving training techniques, studying model behavior or researching more efficient ways to build intelligent systems.

This is one of the more research-intensive careers in AI. Positions at research labs and advanced technology companies can involve significant experimentation, mathematical work and publication-quality research.

**US salary data**

The closest broad BLS occupational category is Computer and Information Research Scientists.

- May 2025 median annual wage: **\$140,300**

- Highest 10% earned more than: **\$230,630**

- Projected employment growth, 2025--2035: **22%**

These figures cover the wider research-scientist occupation, not exclusively AI Research Scientists.

**Skills that influence compensation**

- Advanced mathematics and statistics

- Deep learning and neural networks

- Python and PyTorch

- Model architecture and optimization

- Experimental design

- Research paper analysis and scientific communication

**Education expectations**

Many research scientist positions prefer or require a master\'s degree or PhD, particularly when the work involves original research. Requirements vary between research labs, applied research teams and industry positions.

## **3. AI Architect**

An AI Architect designs the overall technical structure of an organization\'s AI systems.

Instead of focusing on a single model or application, this professional looks at how different components fit together: data sources, models, APIs, cloud infrastructure, security, monitoring and existing enterprise software.

For example, a large financial institution might want to introduce AI assistants across customer support, compliance and internal operations. An AI Architect helps determine how those applications should be designed, connected and governed.

This is generally a senior-level position. Employers often expect substantial experience in software architecture, cloud systems, data platforms and AI implementation.

**What influences compensation?**

- Enterprise architecture experience

- Cloud architecture and infrastructure

- LLM and machine learning integration

- Data architecture

- Security and governance

- Experience delivering large-scale technology projects

**Salary context**

AI Architect does not have a dedicated BLS wage category. Salary estimates vary considerably depending on whether the position is primarily an enterprise architect role, an AI platform architect role or a senior engineering position.

Rather than assigning an unsupported national salary figure, candidates should compare the published ranges in individual job listings and identify the responsibilities behind the title.

## **4. Generative AI Engineer**

Generative AI Engineers build applications that use large language models and other generative models to perform useful tasks.

A company might want an internal assistant that answers employee questions using confidential company documents. Another might need an AI system that summarizes customer conversations, extracts information from contracts or helps developers understand a large codebase.

Building these applications requires more than writing prompts. Engineers need to connect models to data, manage context, evaluate responses and make the system reliable enough for real users.

**What influences compensation?**

- Strong software engineering fundamentals

- Python and LLM APIs

- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

- Embeddings and vector databases

- Tool calling and workflow orchestration

- Model evaluation and observability

- Cloud deployment and application security

**Salary context**

There is no separate BLS salary category for Generative AI Engineers. Some employers advertise these positions under AI Engineer, Software Engineer, Machine Learning Engineer or Applied AI Engineer.

For a useful compensation comparison, candidates can examine the AI/ML Engineer starting-salary estimates published by Robert Half, while checking whether the responsibilities in a specific listing actually match Generative AI application development.

## **5. AI Product Manager**

AI Product Managers decide what AI-powered products should do, which problems they should solve and how their success should be measured.

They work with engineering, design, data, sales and business teams. Their job is to ensure that the technology solves a real customer problem rather than becoming an expensive experiment.

Consider a company introducing an AI-powered customer support platform. The Product Manager may define the product requirements, decide which customer interactions should be automated, work with engineers on model evaluation and measure whether the product improves response times and customer satisfaction.

**What influences compensation?**

- Product management experience

- Understanding of AI and LLM capabilities

- Product strategy and prioritization

- Data-driven decision-making

- Customer research

- Communication and stakeholder management

- Experience launching enterprise software

**Who can move into this role?**

Professionals with backgrounds in product management, business analysis, consulting, technology or operations may be suitable candidates. A deep machine learning background is not required for every AI Product Manager role, but a practical understanding of AI limitations and risks is important.

**Salary context**

AI Product Manager is not a separately reported BLS occupation. Compensation varies with product scope, company size, seniority and whether the position includes technical product ownership.

## **6. AI Security Engineer**

As organizations introduce AI into their internal systems, security teams face risks that traditional application security practices may not fully address.

AI Security Engineers help protect models, AI applications, data and infrastructure. Their work can include testing for prompt injection, preventing unauthorized data access, securing model APIs and assessing vulnerabilities in AI-powered applications.

For example, an AI assistant connected to an organization\'s internal documents must respect user permissions. A poorly designed system could expose information to employees who should not have access to it.

**US salary benchmark**

The BLS reported a May 2025 median annual wage of **\$129,180** for Information Security Analysts.

This is a broad cybersecurity benchmark, not a direct salary figure for AI Security Engineers.

**Skills that influence compensation**

- Cybersecurity and application security

- Cloud security

- API security

- Identity and access management

- Threat modeling

- LLM security and prompt-injection testing

- Data protection and AI governance

Professionals with a strong cybersecurity foundation may find AI security an interesting specialization as organizations expand their use of AI applications.

## **7. Data Scientist**

Data Scientists analyze data, build statistical and predictive models, and help organizations make better decisions.

Their work can involve predicting customer behavior, estimating financial risk, identifying operational problems or understanding how a product is performing.

AI has changed the tools available to Data Scientists, but it has not removed the need for statistical reasoning, data quality and business understanding.

**US salary data**

According to the BLS:

- May 2025 median annual wage: **\$120,230**

- Highest 10% earned more than: **\$199,130**

- Projected employment growth, 2025--2035: **35%**

Robert Half\'s 2026 Salary Guide provides a separate starting-salary estimate for Data Scientists:

  ------------------ ----------------------------
   **Salary level**   **Annual starting salary**

         Low                  \$121,750

         Mid                  \$153,750

         High                 \$182,500
  ------------------ ----------------------------

The BLS median and Robert Half starting-salary ranges come from different methodologies and should not be treated as interchangeable.

**Skills that influence compensation**

Python, SQL, statistics, machine learning, experimentation, data visualization and business analytics.

Data Scientists with experience in a valuable domain---such as finance, healthcare, insurance or technology---may bring additional value because they understand the business problems behind the data.

## **8. Data Engineer**

Data Engineers build the infrastructure that allows companies to collect, process, store and use data.

Their work is particularly important in AI projects. A model cannot produce reliable results if the data feeding it is incomplete, inconsistent or difficult to access.

A Data Engineer may build pipelines that bring together information from customer databases, payment systems, applications and external data sources. Those pipelines support analytics, machine learning and generative AI applications.

**US salary data**

Robert Half\'s 2026 Salary Guide gives the following national starting-salary estimates:

  ------------------ ----------------------------
   **Salary level**   **Annual starting salary**

         Low                  \$127,000

         Mid                  \$156,250

         High                 \$180,750
  ------------------ ----------------------------

**Skills that influence compensation**

- SQL and Python

- Data pipelines and ETL

- Apache Spark

- Airflow and workflow orchestration

- Snowflake and Databricks

- Cloud data platforms

- Data modeling and data quality

Data engineering is not always marketed as an AI career, but it is an important part of the infrastructure behind enterprise AI.

## **9. Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)**

Forward Deployed Engineers work directly with customers to solve technical problems and implement software in real business environments.

They combine software engineering with customer-facing problem-solving. Instead of working exclusively on a product from an internal engineering team, an FDE may spend time understanding a customer\'s operations, building integrations and adapting technology to the customer\'s requirements.

Imagine a logistics company that wants to automate shipment planning. An FDE might work with its operations team, connect internal databases, build an AI-powered application and refine the system based on feedback from employees.

**Skills that influence compensation**

- Strong software engineering

- Python and APIs

- LLM integration and RAG

- Databases and cloud infrastructure

- Debugging and system integration

- Customer communication

- Ability to work through ambiguous problems

**Salary context**

Forward Deployed Engineer is an emerging job title without a separate BLS occupational salary series. Compensation can vary significantly depending on the employer, technical depth, seniority and customer-facing responsibilities.

Candidates should compare actual employer listings and examine whether the role is closer to software engineering, solutions engineering or technical consulting.

## **10. MLOps / AI Infrastructure Engineer**

An AI system that works in a demonstration environment is not necessarily ready for thousands or millions of users.

MLOps and AI Infrastructure Engineers help bridge that gap. They build the systems that deploy, monitor, scale and maintain machine learning and AI applications.

For example, a company running an AI-powered recommendation system needs to monitor performance, update models, manage infrastructure costs and detect problems when the data changes. These engineers help keep the system operating reliably.

**Skills that influence compensation**

- Python and Linux

- Docker and Kubernetes

- CI/CD and infrastructure automation

- Cloud platforms

- Model deployment and monitoring

- MLflow and model versioning

- Distributed systems and data pipelines

**Salary context**

MLOps and AI Infrastructure roles do not have one dedicated BLS salary category. Depending on the job description, they may overlap with software engineering, cloud engineering, DevOps or machine learning engineering.

The most useful comparison is the salary range for the actual responsibilities---not simply the title.

## **Does working at a major technology company change the salary?**

It can make a substantial difference.

Some technology companies offer compensation packages that include:

- Base salary

- Annual performance bonuses

- Restricted stock units (RSUs)

- Signing bonuses

- Other benefits and incentives

This is why two engineers with similar base salaries may have very different total compensation.

For example, Levels.fyi reported the following US AI Engineer compensation distribution on November 1, 2026:

  ----------------------------- -------------------------------
   **Compensation percentile**   **Total annual compensation**

         25th percentile                   \$110,000

             Median                        \$156,000

         75th percentile                   \$212,000

         90th percentile                   \$282,000
  ----------------------------- -------------------------------

These are employee-reported total compensation figures, including base salary, annualized stock and bonus. They are not guaranteed offers or official national wage statistics. Compensation can vary by company, level, location and the employees who submit data.

For someone evaluating an offer, it is worth comparing base salary and total compensation separately. Stock awards may also vest over several years, so the headline value is not necessarily the amount received as cash in the first year.

## **What actually helps candidates reach the higher salary ranges?**

A high salary usually reflects a combination of technical depth, experience, business impact and the ability to deliver reliable systems.

A candidate who can demonstrate a working AI application is in a different position from someone who has only completed a collection of online courses.

Consider two candidates applying for a Generative AI Engineer position.

- Candidate A lists Python, ChatGPT, LangChain and prompt engineering on a résumé.

- Candidate B has built a document assistant that retrieves information from company-style documents, cites its sources, handles unsupported questions and has been deployed through an API.

Candidate B has given the employer something concrete to evaluate.

The same principle applies to other roles. A Data Scientist should be able to explain the decisions behind a model. An MLOps Engineer should demonstrate how a model is deployed and monitored. An AI Product Manager should be able to explain how an AI feature addresses a customer problem and how its success is measured.

The strongest candidates can connect technical work to measurable outcomes.

## **Can international students in the USA pursue high-paying AI jobs?**

International students can pursue AI careers in the United States, but their work authorization needs to be considered alongside their career plans.

F-1 students may be eligible for Curricular Practical Training (CPT) or Optional Practical Training (OPT), subject to the applicable requirements. Students who meet the conditions for the STEM OPT extension may be eligible for an additional 24 months of practical training.

A few points matter:

- The degree program must meet the relevant eligibility requirements.

- Employment under CPT or OPT must comply with the applicable rules.

- STEM OPT has additional employer and training-plan requirements.

- Work authorization and future sponsorship policies vary by employer.

## **Frequently asked questions**

### **What is the highest-paying AI job in the USA?**

There is no definitive national ranking covering every AI job title. Senior research, machine learning, AI architecture and specialized engineering roles can command high compensation. For a comparable official benchmark, the BLS reported a May 2025 median annual wage of \$140,300 for Computer and Information Research Scientists. Some technology-company roles may offer substantially higher total compensation, particularly when stock is included.

### **How much does an AI Engineer earn in the USA?**

Levels.fyi reported a median total compensation of \$156,000 for AI Engineers in the United States as of November 1, 2026. This includes base salary, annualized stock and bonus. Robert Half\'s 2026 starting-salary estimates for AI/ML Engineers range from \$134,000 to \$193,250. These figures use different methodologies and should not be compared as though they were the same salary measure.

### **Do Generative AI Engineers earn more than Machine Learning Engineers?**

There is no reliable universal answer. Both titles cover a wide range of responsibilities, and Generative AI Engineer is not yet a separate BLS occupation. Compensation depends on engineering experience, the complexity of the systems being built, company size and location.

### **Which AI job pays well without requiring a PhD?**

Many AI engineering, data engineering, software engineering and product positions do not require a PhD. Employers generally look for relevant technical ability and experience. Research positions developing new algorithms are more likely to prefer advanced academic qualifications.

### **Can non-technical professionals earn high salaries in AI?**

Yes, although the path is different. AI Product Management, AI consulting and certain AI strategy or business roles can be relevant to professionals with business, product, operations or industry experience. Compensation depends on seniority and the ability to deliver business results, not simply familiarity with AI tools.

### **Are AI jobs in the USA expected to grow in 2027?**

The latest BLS projections available for 2025--2035 show strong projected growth in occupations such as Data Scientists and Computer and Information Research Scientists. These are occupational forecasts, not a direct prediction of the number of AI-specific vacancies in 2027. Job-posting data also show growing employer interest in AI-related skills.

### **Does an AI job always pay more than a regular software engineering job?**

No. Compensation depends on the company, level, location and responsibilities. A senior software engineer at a major technology company may earn more than an entry-level AI Engineer. The value of the work and the candidate\'s experience matter more than the job title alone.

## **Final thoughts**

AI is creating well-paid opportunities, but the highest salaries are usually attached to people who can take responsibility for difficult problems.

That might mean building a machine learning system that works reliably at scale, designing the architecture for an enterprise AI platform, securing sensitive company data or helping a customer integrate AI into a complex business process.

The job title gets attention. The ability to deliver is what makes a candidate valuable.

If you are comparing AI careers in the United States, look beyond headline salary figures. Check the responsibilities in actual job postings, understand whether a compensation figure includes stock and bonuses, and compare your current experience with what employers are asking for.

A realistic view of the market will help you make a better career decision than chasing the highest number you find online.
