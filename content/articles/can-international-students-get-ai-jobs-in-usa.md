# **Can International Students Get AI Jobs in the USA?**

The United States is home to many technology companies, research institutions, startups, and organisations investing in artificial intelligence. As AI adoption expands across industries, students studying computer science, data science, information technology, engineering, and related subjects are exploring opportunities to build careers in this field.

For international students pursuing education in the United States, one question comes up frequently:

**Can international students get AI jobs in the USA after completing their degree?**

Yes. International students can pursue AI-related employment opportunities in the United States. However, getting hired and being legally authorised to work are two separate matters.

Students in F-1 status must understand the employment options available to them, including Curricular Practical Training (CPT), Optional Practical Training (OPT), and the STEM OPT extension, where eligible.

They must also develop the technical skills employers expect from AI professionals. A degree alone may not be enough to stand out in a competitive hiring process.

In this guide, we explore AI career opportunities in the USA, the types of roles international students can target, F-1 employment considerations, employer expectations, and how to prepare for an AI career.

*Important: This article provides general educational information, not individual immigration or legal advice. F-1 employment eligibility depends on the student\'s circumstances, program, school, and applicable regulations. Always confirm your situation with your university\'s Designated School Official (DSO) or a qualified immigration attorney.*

## **1. Can International Students Legally Work in AI Jobs in the USA?**

Yes, international students may be able to work in AI-related positions in the United States when they have the appropriate employment authorisation.

F-1 students have several potential employment pathways, depending on their academic circumstances.

  ------------------------ ---------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   **Employment pathway**           **When it may apply**                                          **Important consideration**
    On-campus employment       During eligible periods of study                 Generally limited to 20 hours per week while school is in session
            CPT                   During an academic program         Must be an integral part of the established curriculum and authorised before work begins
     Pre-completion OPT     Before completing the academic program             Requires applicable OPT authorisation and is subject to restrictions
    Post-completion OPT      After completing an eligible program                  Employment must be directly related to the student\'s major
     STEM OPT extension      After qualifying post-completion OPT          Requires an eligible STEM degree and compliance with additional requirements
  ------------------------ ---------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The availability of these options is not automatic. Students must meet the applicable eligibility requirements and obtain the required authorisation.

### **What is the difference between getting an AI job and being authorised to work?**

Consider two situations.

**Situation A: A student receives an AI internship offer.**

The student is enrolled in a master\'s program and receives an internship offer from a technology company.

The offer itself does not authorise the student to begin working. The student must first determine whether the internship qualifies for CPT or another applicable employment pathway and obtain the required authorisation.

**Situation B: A graduate receives an AI Engineer job offer.**

A student completes a qualifying degree and receives an offer from an AI company.

The graduate may be able to work through post-completion OPT if eligible, provided the employment is directly related to the major and all applicable requirements are met.

The important lesson is simple: an employer\'s willingness to hire you does not replace immigration authorisation.

## **5. Understanding CPT: Can F-1 Students Do AI Internships?**

Curricular Practical Training (CPT) is an employment authorisation option that may allow eligible F-1 students to participate in internships, cooperative education, and other practical training opportunities during their academic program.

For students interested in AI, CPT can be relevant when an internship or practical training opportunity is genuinely part of the academic curriculum.

### **What is required for CPT?**

Under the applicable F-1 rules, CPT must:

- Be an integral part of an established curriculum.

- Be directly related to the student\'s major area of study.

- Be authorised by the student\'s DSO in SEVIS.

- Be approved before the student begins the employment.

- Identify the specific employer and authorised employment period.

A student generally needs to secure the training opportunity before CPT can be authorised.

### **Examples of AI internships that may be relevant**

Depending on the academic program and the DSO\'s assessment, examples could include:

  -------------------------------- ---------------------------------------------
      **Internship position**            **Potential academic connection**

      Machine Learning Intern            Machine Learning or Data Science

   AI Software Engineering Intern    Computer Science or Software Engineering

        Data Science Intern                 Statistics or Data Science

       Computer Vision Intern               Computer Engineering or AI

        Generative AI Intern        Computer Science or Artificial Intelligence
  -------------------------------- ---------------------------------------------

These examples do not establish eligibility. The student\'s actual curriculum and the proposed training must be assessed.

### **Important: CPT is not simply a way to obtain permission to work**

An internship does not qualify for CPT merely because it is related to a student\'s career interests or because an employer is willing to hire them.

The practical training must meet the curriculum-related requirements.

Students should speak with their university\'s international student office before accepting or beginning any internship.

## **6. Can International Students Get AI Jobs Through OPT?**

Yes. Post-completion Optional Practical Training (OPT) is an important pathway through which eligible F-1 students can gain practical work experience after completing their academic programs.

For an AI graduate, OPT may allow employment in a role directly related to their major area of study, subject to the applicable requirements and USCIS authorisation.

### **What is OPT?**

Optional Practical Training allows eligible F-1 students to gain practical experience related to their academic field.

There are two broad types:

- Pre-completion OPT: Practical training undertaken before completing the academic program.

- Post-completion OPT: Practical training undertaken after completing the academic program.

For eligible students, the standard post-completion OPT period is generally up to 12 months at each qualifying educational level.

### **What AI jobs can students pursue on OPT?**

The specific role must be directly related to the student\'s major.

Examples of potentially relevant employment include:

  ------------------------- ---------------------------
     **Academic major**      **Example AI employment**

      Computer Science         AI Software Engineer

        Data Science         Machine Learning Engineer

   Artificial Intelligence          AI Engineer

    Computer Engineering     Computer Vision Engineer

         Statistics           Applied Data Scientist

    Software Engineering      Generative AI Developer
  ------------------------- ---------------------------

These are illustrative examples. The student\'s actual degree coursework, job duties, and circumstances matter.

### **Is a job offer required before applying for OPT?**

For an initial post-completion OPT application, a student generally does not need to have secured a job offer before applying.

However, students must follow the required application process, deadlines, and employment rules. They should coordinate with their DSO and review the current USCIS Form I-765 instructions.

Official reference: [[USCIS -- Optional Practical Training for F-1 Students]{.underline}](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/students-and-exchange-visitors/optional-practical-training-opt-for-f-1-students).

## **7. Can AI Graduates Qualify for the STEM OPT Extension?**

Potentially, yes.

Eligible F-1 students may apply for a 24-month STEM OPT extension after their initial post-completion OPT period.

However, the extension is not automatically available to everyone who studies AI or works in an AI-related position.

Eligibility depends on specific requirements, including the qualifying degree and employer arrangements.

### **What determines STEM OPT eligibility?**

Important considerations include:

1.  The student must meet the applicable F-1 and OPT requirements.

2.  The qualifying degree must be included on the DHS STEM Designated Degree Program List.

3.  The degree\'s classification and CIP code must meet the relevant requirements.

4.  The employer must satisfy applicable STEM OPT requirements, including E-Verify participation.

5.  The student and employer must complete the required Form I-983 training plan.

6.  The employment must meet the relevant training, supervision, and relationship-to-degree requirements.

### **Which AI-related degrees may qualify?**

Some academic programs that may be relevant to STEM fields include:

- Computer Science

- Artificial Intelligence

- Data Science

- Machine Learning

- Computer Engineering

- Statistics

- Information Technology

But the program name alone is not sufficient to determine eligibility.

**Check the actual CIP code associated with your degree against the current DHS STEM Designated Degree Program List.** Two programs with similar names may have different classifications.

### **What is Form I-983?**

Form I-983 is the Training Plan for STEM OPT Students.

It is completed by the student and employer and describes the training opportunity, learning objectives, supervision, and evaluation process.

The training must be structured and directly related to the qualifying STEM degree.

### **Can international students work in AI startups?**

Potentially, yes, if the employment arrangement satisfies the applicable authorisation requirements. Students should pay particular attention to the employer, training structure, supervision, and other requirements relevant to their specific employment pathway.

### **Is a master\'s degree necessary to get an AI job in the USA?**

Not every AI position requires a master\'s degree. Requirements vary by role. Some positions value bachelor\'s degrees and practical experience, while specialised research and advanced ML roles may prefer graduate-level education.

## **Final Thoughts: Build AI Skills While Planning Your Career Path Carefully**

International students can pursue meaningful AI careers in the United States. The opportunities span software development, generative AI, machine learning, data engineering, automation, research, and enterprise implementation.

You need to understand the technologies employers use, develop practical engineering skills, build a portfolio, prepare for interviews, and understand the employment authorisation requirements that apply to your situation.

A good starting point is to identify the AI role you want to pursue and work backwards:

2.  Identify the skills you need to develop.

3.  Build practical projects.

4.  Prepare your resume and professional profile.

5.  Understand your CPT, OPT, or STEM OPT options with your university.

6.  Apply to relevant opportunities when you are appropriately authorised.
